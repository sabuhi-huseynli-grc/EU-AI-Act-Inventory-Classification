# EU AI Act System Inventory & Risk Classification Engine

> **A standardized enterprise registry and classification methodology mapping corporate AI deployments to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act).**

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Under the EU AI Act, organizations must maintain rigorous visibility over their AI systems, distinguishing between **Providers** (developers/packagers) and **Deployers** (users/operators). 

This repository provides an enterprise-ready **AI System Inventory Register** and **Article 5 Prohibited Practice Screener** designed to classify systems into four distinct risk tiers, identify mandatory governance obligations, and prevent illegal deployments prior to market placement.

---

## 🛠 Portfolio Artifacts

1. **`Prohibited_AI_Practices_Checklist.md`**
   * Pre-deployment screening checklist mapping directly to **Article 5 (1)(a)–(h)** prohibitions (e.g., emotion recognition in workplaces, untargeted facial scraping, cognitive manipulation).
2. **`EU_AI_System_Inventory.csv`**
   * Multi-column tracking register capturing System ID, Organizational Role (Provider vs. Deployer), Risk Classification, Legal Basis, Transparency Obligations, and Compliance Status.

## ⚖️ Regulatory Tier Classification Breakdown

| Risk Tier | EU AI Act Reference | Scope / Examples | Mandatory Obligations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 4: Prohibited Risk** | Article 5 | Emotion recognition in workplace/education, social scoring, dark-pattern manipulation. | **Hard Stop:** Immediate deployment rejection. |
| **Tier 3: High Risk** | Article 6 & Annex III | HR/Recruiting AI, Credit Scoring, Critical Infrastructure, Biometric identification. | Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA), Quality Management System (QMS), CE Marking, Human Oversight. |
| **Tier 2: Limited Risk** | Article 50 | Conversational chatbots, synthetic media generation, generative AI text engines. | User transparency disclosures, watermarking AI-generated outputs. |
| **Tier 1: Minimal Risk** | Article 95 | Internal code completion plugins, spam filters, search ranking algorithms. | Voluntary Codes of Conduct; Standard IT Acceptable Use Policies. |
